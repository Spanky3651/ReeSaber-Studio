Quick Start - how to build a ReeSaber preset in 4 steps
1
Pick a starter

Use New ▾ → template for a ready-made saber, or start blank. Each preset is one saber config file.

2
Stack modules

Add a Blade, then layer Trails and Sparks. Select a Group or Dynamic Joint first to nest layers inside it. Click a layer's dot to mute it.

3
Tune & drive

Open the accordions to shape mesh, material and color. Add Drivers to make a layer react to Score, Velocity, cuts & more.

4
Check & export

Watch the Live JSON and Validate tabs. When it's green, hit Export (.json or .reesaber). Your work autosaves in this browser.

What's New
6 updates

    

      
Curve and gradient editors
Drag points to shape the blade profile and every mapping curve, and paint color gradients along the blade or trail. Open a layer's Blade Shape Curves or Colors & Mappings panel.

      
Swing test
Slash the sabers in the preview so trails streak, sparks burst on cuts and dynamic joints jiggle. Turn it on above the 3D view, next to the backdrop and color pickers.

      
Snippets and layer tools
Drop in a hilt, guard ring, dangling charm, double-ended staff or outline glow. Duplicate, copy, paste, solo and drag layers into groups from the ⋯ menu.

      
Undo and checkpoints
Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z, plus named checkpoints so you can try something wild and roll back. The buttons sit next to the editor tabs.

      
Share, gallery and capture
Copy a share link from Export, open community presets from New Preset ▾ → Browse the gallery, and save a PNG or looping GIF of your saber with Capture.

      
Drop files anywhere
Drag a .reesaber, .json or .zip onto the page to import it. In Chrome or Edge you can also save straight into your Presets folder from Export.
