Quick Start - how to build a ReeSaber preset in 4 steps New to ReeSabers?
1
Pick a starter

Use New Preset ▾ to open a community saber from the gallery or a template, then make it yours. Each preset is one saber file.

2
Stack layers

Use Add layer for a Blade, then Trails and Sparks. Select a Group or Dynamic Joint first to nest layers inside it.

3
Tune & drive

Open the panels to shape mesh, material and color. Add Drivers to make a layer react to score, swing speed and more.

4
Check & export

When Validate is green, hit Export, or Quest to push it to your headset over USB. Your work autosaves in this browser.

New to ReeSabers? Read the Guide
The Guide button explains what ReeSabers is, how to get it, and how to get your preset into the game on PC or Quest, including copying files to the headset.

      
Simple and Advanced
Settings now use plain names like Thickness (cm) and Edge glow, and every one has a ? explaining it. The technical ones hide until you flip on Advanced next to the editor tabs.

      
More room for the saber
The preview buttons fit on one row, with backdrop, colors and camera under View, so the 3D view is much bigger on laptops.

      
One-click export
Export has one main button, Download for Beat Saber, plus where to put the file and how to pick it in game.
