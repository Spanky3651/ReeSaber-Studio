Quick Start — how to build a ReeSaber preset in 4 steps
1
Pick a starter

Use New ▾ → template for a ready-made saber, or start blank. Each preset is one saber config file.

2
Stack modules

Add a Blade, then layer Trails and Sparks. Select a Group or Dynamic Joint first to nest layers inside it. Click a layer's dot to mute it.

3
Tune & drive

Open the accordions to shape mesh, material and color. Add Drivers to make a layer react to Score, Velocity, cuts & more.

4
Check & export

Watch the Live JSON and Validate tabs. When it's green, hit Export (.json or .reesaber). Your work autosaves in this browser.
